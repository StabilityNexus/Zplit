KYA - Know Your Assumptions

What is KYA?

KYA (Know Your Assumptions) is The Stable Order's commitment to transparency and user safety. We believe users should understand the assumptions, limitations, and risks of the tools they use.

Zplit Assumptions:

  • Your device is secure and not compromised
  • P2P connections (WiFi Direct, Bluetooth, NFC) are available
  • Users manually verify shared data accuracy
  • No central server means no remote backup
  • Local data can be lost if device is lost/damaged

Privacy & Security:

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • No personal data is collected or transmitted to servers
  • P2P communication is encrypted during transfer
  • You are responsible for backing up your data

For more information, visit our GitHub repository.
